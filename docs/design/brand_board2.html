Swasth: name and logo ideas

Each mark is drawn as one idea, in two colours only (charcoal and gold). Shown big, small (as in the menu) and on the dark menu.

1
Swasthya
Ayurveda clinic software
"Swasthya" = health. The letter S is one flowing line (like a pulse and a river), ending in a gold dot (bindu).
Swasthya
2
Swastha
Ayurveda clinic software
"Swastha" = a healthy, balanced person. Two leaves turned half a circle against each other make an S, with a gold dot where they meet (balance).
Swastha
3
SwasthVed
Ayurveda clinic software
"SwasthVed" = Swasth + Veda (knowledge). A white leaf with a gold pulse line through it: Ayurveda + check-up.
SwasthVed
4
Swasthi
Ayurveda clinic software
"Swasthi" = well-being. An open ring (care that is never closed) with a small gold leaf growing out of it.
